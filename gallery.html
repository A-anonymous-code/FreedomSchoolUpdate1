Annual Association of Model Islamic School, Lagelu Chapter, 2024 Children's Day Celebration

Our students recently enjoyed an exciting and educational trip to Ilaji Farms and Resorts, where learning met fun in a vibrant outdoor setting. The visit gave the children a chance to explore nature, see farm animals up close, and understand basic agricultural practices in a real-life environment. From interactive activities to guided tours around the resort, the experience sparked curiosity and encouraged teamwork among the students. It was a joyful day filled with laughter, discovery, and unforgettable moments.

The Parent Teacher Association (PTA) plays a vital role in fostering a strong partnership between parents, teachers, and the school community.
